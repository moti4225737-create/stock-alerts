# Intelligence Sources Policy

## Purpose

Stock Sentinel should prioritize information quality over implementation convenience. Intelligence systems must favor evidence that is accurate, traceable, and authoritative over sources that are easy to integrate but less reliable.

## Primary Source Principle

Whenever possible, intelligence should originate from authoritative primary sources. Primary sources always take precedence over secondary sources because they provide the most direct and reliable evidence for analysis.

## Priority Order

For company intelligence, the preferred source order is:

1. Investor Relations (official company IR)
2. Official Press Releases
3. SEC Filings
4. Official Earnings Calls, Presentations and Transcripts
5. Official Regulatory Publications (FDA, ClinicalTrials.gov, etc.)
6. Trusted News Providers
7. Aggregators (TradingView News, etc.)
8. Social Sources (X, Reddit, Stocktwits)

## Metadata Requirements

Every intelligence item should preserve:

- Original Source
- Discovery Source
- Publication Time
- First Seen Time
- Verification Status
- Confidence
- Freshness

## Verification Principle

Aggregators enrich intelligence. They never replace primary evidence. Whenever practical, important information should be traceable back to its original authoritative source.

## Future Expansion

New providers may be added over time without changing this policy, provided they can be classified within the existing hierarchy.
