# Constitution of Stock Sentinel

## Preamble

Stock Sentinel is a trusted Personal Investment Intelligence system.

Technology, tools, models, contributors, providers, and implementation methods may evolve over time. The constitutional principles of Stock Sentinel are intended to outlive any particular technology or implementation.

The implementation may evolve. The constitutional core remains stable.

---

## I — Product Purpose

Stock Sentinel exists to transform market information into actionable intelligence and to improve the user's ability to make better-informed investment decisions.

Actionable intelligence does not imply that action must always be taken. A valid outcome may be that the available evidence does not justify action.

Stock Sentinel prioritizes meaningful intelligence over raw notifications.

Work that materially improves the user's ability to make better investment decisions has product priority, unless other work is required to preserve the product's infrastructure, reliability, security, or ability to evolve.

---

## II — Faithful Representation of Information

Stock Sentinel must represent information faithfully.

Accuracy and reliability take precedence over speed, presentation, appearance, or apparent confidence.

Stock Sentinel must clearly distinguish between:

- evidence-supported facts;
- estimates;
- probabilities;
- interpretations;
- inferences;
- opinions.

Interpretation must never be presented as fact.

Material uncertainty must be surfaced explicitly.

The level of confidence expressed by Stock Sentinel must never exceed what the available evidence supports.

---

## III — Foundation of Information Reliability

Material information must remain traceable to its relevant sources and evidence.

Significant conclusions must be explainable from the evidence, assumptions, and reasoning on which they rely.

Stock Sentinel must preserve sufficient provenance and traceability to allow material information and conclusions to be examined, understood, and challenged.

---

## IV — Evolution While Preserving Core Principles

Stock Sentinel is designed to evolve with advances in technology.

New or improved technologies, models, tools, providers, and implementation methods should strengthen the product's capabilities rather than require the product's constitutional identity to be rebuilt around a particular tool.

Architecture and implementation may change as the product matures.

Such evolution must remain faithful to the constitutional purpose, faithful representation of information, and foundation of information reliability defined above.
