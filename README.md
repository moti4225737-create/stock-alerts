# Stock Sentinel

Stock Sentinel is a Personal Investment Intelligence system designed to transform market information into trustworthy, source-grounded, portfolio-relevant intelligence.

The product is not a generic news feed or alert bot. Its purpose is to detect meaningful developments, ground material facts in authoritative evidence, correlate related events, explain their significance, evaluate portfolio relevance, and present concise intelligence to the user.
