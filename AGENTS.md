## Mandatory Working Rule

Before making a material architectural, domain-contract, engineering-governance, access-governance, or documentation-structure decision:

1. Determine purpose and essence.
2. Map the existing system or process end-to-end.
3. Test the normal scenario and relevant failure/abuse scenario.
4. Benchmark professional analogues when materially useful.
5. Present a reasoned proposal.
6. Obtain product-owner approval where required.
7. Implement only after the applicable approval and validation gates.
